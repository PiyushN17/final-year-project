Why this project is useful

Farm information is often spread across different websites and may be difficult to understand. This project keeps common farming tools together and gives results in a simpler form.

  • 86% of operational landholders in India are small or marginal. Government source
  • Plant pests and diseases can cause losses of up to 40% of global crop production. FAO source
  • Agriculture supports 46.1% of employment in India. Economic Survey reference
